Welcome to **BetterMovies** — an open-source media discovery and streaming site where you can watch your favorite movies and shows for free, specializing in **1080p Open Matte Format** releases. 

When your favorite movies (like *Spider-Man*) unexpectedly disappear from major streaming platforms like Prime Video, Netflix, or Hulu, BetterMovies acts as your reliable backup to find and enjoy them.

## 🌟 Features

* **High-Quality Playback:** Focused on providing 1080p quality streams.
* **Open Matte Formats:** Experience films without the standard cinematic black bars, utilizing the full screen area.
* **Simple, Lightweight Interface:** Built to be fast, responsive, and easy to navigate.
* **Open-Source:** Fully open-source and community-driven.

## ⚖️ Legal Disclaimer

**BetterMovies does not host, store, or transmit any movies, TV shows, or other media files on our own infrastructure.** 

This service acts strictly as a directory and discovery platform, helping users find and access content provided through third-party sources. We do not have control over the media content, and any copyright claims or takedown requests should be directed to the respective external hosts.
