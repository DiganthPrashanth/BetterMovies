DevEvil (FOSS streaming site)

DevEvil is an alternative streaming platform you can visit if BetterMovies doesn't have the movie you're looking for.

Although it is also Free and Open-Source, there may be trackers such as static.cloudflareinsights.com on the page.

For Safety reasons, we recommend using a ad/content blocker such as uBlock Origin or NextDns,

And use a Vpn like ProtonVPN to maximize your privacy.

If for any reason the Video stream doesnt load on DevEvil, you should disable your ad blocker and try again,

as the site sometimes depends on trackers
